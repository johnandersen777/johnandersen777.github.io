    • Mr. Azzarello's writing uses the phrase "So we would believe it" repeatedly, this literary device is not literal, it is metaphorical for the mindset we must avoid falling into, this is the vulnerability we must collectively overcome which is feed to us via ingrained biases and cultural norms.
